15 Consolidated Services
280+ Impacted Clients
200K+ Calls/Month

Project Description

Leading the strategy for unifying the fraud prevention portfolio, consolidating 15 services (fraud score, biometric analysis, background check, among others) into 2 integrated portals with a single interface.
